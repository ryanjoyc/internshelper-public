# Unslop

Make the writing sound like a specific person communicating a specific result.

## Edit

- Lead with the point. Delete generic openings, reassurance, praise, and conclusions.
- Replace puffery, vague attribution, and promotional language with concrete facts or remove them.
- Prefer plain verbs: use, make, fix, move, delete, and check.
- Remove filler, repeated conclusions, forced groups of three, synonym cycling, and empty transition words.
- Use sentence-case headings and only as much formatting as the content needs.
- Reduce em dashes, decorative colons, parenthetical detours, bold labels, and decorative emoji.
- Break dense sentences. Keep one main thought in each sentence and vary rhythm naturally.
- Prefer active voice when the actor matters.
- Replace abstract technical metaphors with the real mechanism, symbol, file, command, number, or failure.
- Keep uncertainty that matters. Do not turn a qualified result into a confident claim.

Preserve required facts, citations, caveats, decisions, and next steps. Add voice through judgment and specificity, not jokes or manufactured informality. Finish by asking what still makes the text sound generated, then fix only the real tells.
